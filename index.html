Questions

How is the forecast calculated?

It starts from your current bank balance, adds invoices on their due dates and subtracts recurring bills and one-off costs you enter. It does not guess when clients will pay; you can shift a client's date to see the effect.

Does Ledgerline connect to my bank?

Yes, through bank feeds from supported Indian banks. You can also upload a statement file if your bank is not supported.

Can I cancel at any time?

Yes. Monthly plans stop at the end of the billing month. Yearly plans are refunded for unused full months.
